# Analysis Rules

Findings must be generated from evidence.

AI is responsible for explanation, not detection.

Deterministic metric definitions and the shared coverage/eligibility rules live in [P3 核心指标、KAST / Trade / Clutch 与覆盖机制](./analytics-metrics.md). Findings build on those computed metrics; they do not re-derive coverage.

P4.1 rules and their exact evidence/sample/coverage thresholds are documented in [Findings Engine](./findings-engine.md). Implemented categories: side impact, traded-death rate, opening performance, direct utility impact, teammate flash effects, and clutch highlights.

Low-impact/consistency, movement/first-shot behavior, positioning and actual blind-time efficiency require reliable future Analytics evidence; they are not inferred from this single-match MVP.
